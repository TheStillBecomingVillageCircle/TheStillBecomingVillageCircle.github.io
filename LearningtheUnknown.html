The Unknown

What if you don't need to know yet?

Sometimes the next version of you is still becoming clear.

There is something beautiful about not knowing.

We are often taught to search for the answer before we have even given ourselves permission to ask the question.

But becoming doesn't always arrive with instructions.

Sometimes you are simply standing at the edge of something unfamiliar, wondering what happens next.

That doesn't mean you're lost.

It might mean you're discovering.

Questions Worth Sitting With

You don't have to answer these today. Some questions are meant to open a door, not force you through it.

QUESTION ONE

What are you curious about right now?

Follow the question before you worry about where it leads.

QUESTION TWO

What part of yourself are you still getting to know?

You are allowed to meet yourself again.

QUESTION THREE

What would you explore if you knew you didn't have to get it right?

Curiosity doesn't require perfection.

You Are Allowed to Be Unsure.

You can change your mind.

You can take a different route.

You can discover that what you wanted isn't what you want anymore.

You can begin again.

And you can do all of that without explaining your becoming to everybody.

The unknown isn't always a warning. Sometimes it's an invitation.

👣 Keep Walking.

You don't have to see the whole path to take the next step.

Take what you need. Leave what you don't. Come back whenever you need to.

Healing isn't a destination. It's a circle we grow through together.
