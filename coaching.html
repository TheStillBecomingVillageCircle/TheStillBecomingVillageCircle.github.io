Support

A space to pause, unpack, question, reflect, and explore what becoming looks like from where you are right now.

You Don't Have to Figure It Out Alone.

Sometimes you don't need someone to tell you what to do.

You need somewhere safe enough to slow down and hear yourself think.

Coaching inside The Still Becoming Village Circle creates space for honest presence, curiosity, reflection, and intentional movement.

You bring what you're carrying. We explore it together.

What Can We Explore?

Your becoming doesn't have to fit into one perfect category.

Coaching may create space to explore:

  • A transition you're trying to understand
  • A decision you're struggling to make
  • Boundaries you're learning to establish
  • Relationships and communication
  • Identity and self-discovery
  • Creative ideas you're trying to bring into form
  • Patterns you're beginning to notice
  • The version of yourself you're becoming
  • Something you simply need to say out loud

You don't have to know exactly what you're becoming before you begin.

Eudaimonia & Liminality

👂 Listen

The first responsibility is to hear what you're actually saying—not simply prepare a response.

🔎 Explore

Questions can help uncover what is underneath the immediate problem, assumption, fear, desire, or decision.

🪞 Reflect

Reflection creates room to notice patterns, beliefs, choices, and possibilities without rushing toward an answer.

🌱 Become

The goal isn't to become somebody else. It's to become more intentional about who you are becoming.

Eudaimonia & Liminality Are Not a Diagnosis.

Coaching is a space for reflection, personal development, and exploring possibilities.

It is not therapy, medical care, crisis intervention, or a substitute for licensed mental-health treatment.

If something requires specialized professional care, that deserves the appropriate professional support.
