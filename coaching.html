Kendra's Coaching Space

Instantiator of Becoming

A space to slow down, examine what you're carrying, ask better questions, and explore what you're becoming.

Coaching Isn't About Fixing You.

You are not a problem waiting for somebody else to solve.

Sometimes you simply need enough space to hear yourself think without everybody else's expectations talking over you.

My role is not to become the voice of your life. It's to help you hear your own voice more clearly.

What We Can Explore

🪞

Reflection

What are you noticing about yourself, your relationships, your choices, or the season you're currently in?

🧭

Rerouting

Sometimes the direction that once made sense doesn't anymore. We can explore what changed and what might come next.

🌱

Becoming

Who are you becoming when you stop organizing your life around who everyone else needs you to be?

This Is Not a Place to Perform.

You don't need to show up with the perfect story.

You don't need to already know what your next move is.

You don't need to convince me that you're doing okay.

You can simply show up as the person who is trying to understand what happens next.

This Space May Be for You If...

You're somewhere between knowing something needs to change and figuring out what that change actually looks like.

🌱 You're entering a new season of life.
🪞 You're questioning patterns you've outgrown.
🧭 You're considering a different direction.
💬 You need somewhere to think out loud.
🫧 You're learning how to create healthier boundaries.
✨ You know you're becoming someone different.
