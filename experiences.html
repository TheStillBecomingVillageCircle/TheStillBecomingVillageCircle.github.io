Experiences

Becoming is something you experience.

The Still Becoming Village Circle isn't meant to live only on a screen. Some things are meant to be felt, explored, shared, created, and remembered.

More Than Something to Look At.

Experiences are opportunities to step outside of simply consuming information and actually participate in something.

A conversation. A gathering. A workshop. A creative moment. A reflection. A room full of people discovering that they aren't the only ones figuring life out.

Some experiences will happen virtually. Some may happen in person. Some may be quiet. Some may be loud.

The common thread is simple: you get to experience the Village, not just read about it.

What Might Happen Here

🧠

Workshops

Interactive spaces for learning, reflection, conversation, and practical exploration.

Come ready to question something, learn something, or see something differently.

🫂

Village Gatherings

Spaces to connect with other people who are also navigating the beautiful, complicated process of becoming.

No performance required. Just presence.

🎨

Creative Experiences

Art, writing, décor, storytelling, books, visual experiences, and other ways of making an idea tangible.

Sometimes creativity says what ordinary conversation cannot.

💬

Real Conversations

Conversations about the things people don't always know how to say out loud.

Curiosity belongs here. So does honesty.

The Village Is Still Becoming.

Some experiences are already taking shape. Others haven't been created yet.

That's intentional.

This Village is allowed to grow organically. New conversations can become new gatherings. New ideas can become new experiences. And something that doesn't exist today may become exactly what someone needs tomorrow.

🗓️ What's Coming

Upcoming Village experiences, workshops, gatherings, and special events will live here as they are created.

Check back as the Village continues to take shape.
