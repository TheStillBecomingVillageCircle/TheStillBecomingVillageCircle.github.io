🌱 Who We Are

The Still Becoming Village Circle was created for people who are figuring things out while they're figuring themselves out.

You don't have to arrive with all the answers. You don't have to know exactly where you're going. You don't even have to know what you're looking for yet.

This is a place for curiosity, reflection, connection, creativity, and growth.

A place where becoming doesn't have to be rushed.

🌱 Our Mission

We believe people deserve space to explore who they are becoming without being pressured to have everything figured out.

The Village creates room for learning, honest conversations, creativity, meaningful experiences, and connection.

Sometimes becoming requires information. Sometimes it requires reflection. Sometimes it requires community. Sometimes it simply requires someone saying, “You can take your time.”

You don't have to know the whole path. You just have to be willing to take the next step.
