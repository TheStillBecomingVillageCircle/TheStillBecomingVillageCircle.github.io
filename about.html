Inside the Bubble

The Still Becoming Village Circle was created for people who are figuring things out while they're figuring themselves out.

A place where becoming doesn't have to be rushed.

The Unknown

The unknown isn't empty. It can be a question, a possibility, a place you've never been, or a version of yourself you've never met.

You don't have to understand everything before you're allowed to explore it.

Sometimes learning begins with admitting that you don't know.

Here, curiosity gets to live without immediately becoming an answer. We can ask questions before we decide what we believe.

Not knowing something doesn't make you unintelligent. Being willing to learn something new doesn't make everything you previously believed meaningless.

Sometimes wisdom begins with: “I don't know yet.”
