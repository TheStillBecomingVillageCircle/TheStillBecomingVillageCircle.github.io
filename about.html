Inside the Bubble

The Still Becoming Village Circle was created for people who are figuring things out while they're figuring themselves out.

You don't have to arrive with all the answers—or know exactly where you're going.

This is a place for curiosity, reflection, connection, creativity, and growth. A place where becoming doesn't have to be rushed.

The Unknown

The unknown isn't empty. It can be a question, a possibility, a place you've never been, or a version of yourself you've never met.

You don't have to understand everything before you're allowed to explore it.

Here, curiosity gets to live without immediately becoming an answer. Sometimes wisdom begins with: “I don't know yet.”

What We Explore

Understanding Yourself

Patterns, perspectives, emotions, assumptions, choices, and questions that help you understand yourself more honestly.

Human Connection

Communication, relationships, boundaries, presence, conflict, curiosity, and what happens between people in real time.

Becoming

The space between who you were, who you are, and who you may be becoming.

Better Questions

Some questions matter because they change the way you look. Learning resources can help the Village keep exploring.

You Are Allowed to Change Your Mind.

Learning means encountering information that may challenge something you thought you already understood.

That doesn't automatically mean you were foolish before. It means you encountered something new.

Curiosity before certainty. Questions before assumptions. Understanding before judgment.
