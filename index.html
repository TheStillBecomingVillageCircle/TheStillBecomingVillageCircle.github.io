Liminality

noun — the state of being between one stage, condition, identity, or place and another.

Sometimes becoming doesn't look like having everything figured out.

Sometimes it looks like standing in the space between what was and what hasn't arrived yet.

That space has a name.

Liminality.

The threshold. The in-between. The place where something is changing before you can fully name what it is.

You don't have to rush through it.
