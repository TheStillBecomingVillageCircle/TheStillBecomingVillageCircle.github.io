🫧 Step Inside the Bubble
Liminality
noun — the state of being between one stage, condition, identity, or place and another

The space between who you were and who you're becoming.

Sometimes an old version of your life no longer fits, while the next version hasn't fully taken shape.

That space can feel uncertain. Strange. Quiet. Exciting. Uncomfortable. Liberating.

And sometimes it can feel like nothing is happening— while everything is changing.

You may not be lost. You may simply be in between.

This Village lives in that space.
