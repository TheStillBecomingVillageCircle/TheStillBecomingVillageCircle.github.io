liminality — the state of being in between one stage, condition, identity, or place and another.

The space between who you were and who you're becoming.

This Village lives in that space.

What Is Liminality?

liminality

Liminality describes the space between what was and what comes next.

It can happen when an old identity no longer fits, but the new one hasn't fully taken shape.

It can feel uncertain. Strange. Quiet. Exciting. Uncomfortable. Liberating.

And sometimes it can feel like absolutely nothing is happening— while everything is changing.

You may not be lost. You may simply be in between.
