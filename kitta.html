Kitta's Creative Space

Creative & Marketing Collaborator

Some things are difficult to explain until someone understands the feeling underneath them. This is a space for turning that feeling into something you can see, hold, give, remember, or experience.

Creativity Begins With the Feeling.

Before the colors, materials, decorations, baskets, memorial pieces, or finished design, there is a person with a reason.

Kitta's process begins by listening.

The goal isn't simply to make something that looks beautiful. It's to understand what the person is trying to express—and then create from there.

Sometimes the most important part of a creative request is the part that never made it onto the original list.

2

3

4

Create

The idea becomes a creative direction, and the creative direction becomes something tangible.

The Form Starts the Conversation.

It isn't meant to replace the human part of the process.

Kitta wants to understand the person behind the request—not just the shopping list.

The initial conversation gives her room to listen to the details, notice the emotion behind the request, and use her creative intuition to determine how the finished piece should feel.

Sometimes you know exactly what you want. Sometimes you only know how you want it to feel. Both are enough to begin.

The Creative Order Cycle

Kitta intentionally works in creative cycles so there is enough time to listen, prepare, create, complete, and deliver each order without rushing the process.

🗓️ Order Days

Orders are accepted on:

Monday • Wednesday • Friday

These are the days to submit a creative request for the current ordering cycle.

🧺 Creation Week

After the ordering window closes, Kitta uses the following week to prepare, complete, and deliver the accepted creative requests.

Then the next ordering cycle opens.
